## Development Tasks

### Suggested — Site Polish & Growth, not started
*Added 2026-08-17 at the owner's direct request: "how to improve my site so it looks more attractive, what small features attract more people, what makes people spend more time." A new bucket, separate from Phase 2 (which is specifically about what end-user accounts unlock) — this one is about visual polish, virality, and time-on-site, and doesn't require accounts to start most of it. Ordered highest priority first — cheapest-to-build, highest-leverage ideas on top. Each item is tagged with its primary goal: **[Look]** = makes the site feel more polished/alive, **[Attract]** = brings new people in, **[Retain]** = keeps existing visitors around longer.*

- [ ] **[Look] Festive/seasonal homepage banner** (Diwali, Holi, New Year, Republic Day, etc.) — a lightweight, date-triggered banner (+ a matching seeded quiz/post around the festival), reusing the exact "deterministic by date, no cron job" pattern Horoscope and Daily Quiz already use. Gives the site a "someone's actively running this" feel around real Indian calendar moments, at near-zero engineering cost.
- [ ] **[Retain] "Random content" button** in the header/footer — one click to a random quiz, post, game, story, or puzzle, for pure serendipitous browsing. Cheap (client-side pick from an already-fetched or one-shot-fetched list) and a direct pages-per-session lever.
- [ ] **[Attract] Embeddable mini-quiz widget** — a stripped-down, chromeless `<iframe>`-embeddable version of a quiz that other blogs/sites can drop in, crediting/linking back to Twegle. Reuses the existing public quiz-play flow; the actual new work is just a minimal layout variant plus embed-friendly headers.
- [ ] **[Retain] First-visit onboarding tour** — a brief 3-4 step tooltip walkthrough pointing a brand-new visitor at Quizzes/Games/Puzzles/Streaks, shown once (localStorage-gated, same pattern as other one-time UI). Today a first-time visitor who takes one quiz has no signal that Games/Puzzles/Stories/Horoscope even exist unless they notice the tabs themselves.
- [ ] **[Monetize] Personality-to-product affiliate recommendations** — added 2026-08-19. On a quiz result page ("You're a Dreamer!"), show 2-3 affiliate-linked products that actually fit that personality type, with a plain "may earn a commission" disclosure (standard FTC-style affiliate disclosure — the same legal requirement every affiliate marketer already follows). Stronger than generic display ads since it's contextual: someone just told they're "a Dreamer" is primed to click a Dreamer-themed product. **Blocked on "Apply to affiliate programs" above** (a real affiliate account is needed before this can be built against a real link format) — and honestly, like every other monetization path here, it won't produce real income until the site has real traffic; building this now would just mean it's ready to switch on the moment both the affiliate account and real visitors exist.
- [ ] **[Retain] Weekly "Champion of the Week" leaderboard** — added 2026-08-19. A leaderboard (per game, or site-wide by points) that resets every week, with a shareable "🏆 Champion" badge for whoever's on top at reset — deliberately **no cash prizes, ever**, just bragging rights and a shareable badge. No-cash-prize is a deliberate legal-safety choice: a real-money prize tied to a game of skill/chance can trigger sweepstakes/contest law depending on the state/country, which a pure on-site cosmetic reward avoids entirely. Reuses the existing leaderboard infrastructure (`GameLeaderboard.jsx`/global leaderboard) with an added weekly-reset dimension.
- [ ] **[Attract] User-generated "Create Your Own Quiz"** — added 2026-08-19. Lets a logged-in visitor build their own simple personality quiz (a stripped-down version of the admin quiz builder) and share it. The single biggest organic-growth lever quiz sites have historically used — people share quizzes they made about themselves or their friend group far more than generic content, since it's personal. Needs a moderation step before anything a visitor built goes fully public (ties directly into the existing "Community-submitted content with a moderation queue" backlog item below — this would essentially be that queue's first real use case) and collects no personal info from creators, consistent with the site's no-email/no-phone approach throughout.
- [ ] **[Look] Translate site-wide CTA button labels in Hindi mode** — added 2026-08-20, found during a QA sweep. In Hindi mode, every card's action-button text ("Take the quiz →", "Play now →", "Fill it in →", "Solve it →", "🔊 Read & Listen →", "Today's Horoscope →") stays hardcoded in English while the actual title/description content is properly translated — a consistent, site-wide gap, not a one-off bug in any single card. Needs a small `language`-aware label per card component (`QuizCard.jsx`, `GameCard.jsx`, `FriendshipQuizCard.jsx`, `PuzzleCard.jsx`, `StoryCard.jsx`, `ZodiacCard.jsx`), all fixed together so no card is left inconsistent with the rest.

**User-side**
- [ ] **Bookmarks / Favorites** — save a quiz, post, or story to revisit later, tied to the account (guests keep working exactly as they do today; this is additive, same as everything else account-related). Highest priority here: small, self-contained, no external dependency, and a clear return-visit hook now that accounts exist.
- [ ] Push notifications (if PWA installed) — the account/subscription piece now exists to make this worth more than a generic broadcast; still needs real push infrastructure (web push + a service worker handler) to be built.

Two near-identical images side by side, 5-10 deliberate differences to spot. Fits the same admin-authored-content model as the riddle puzzles (each puzzle = 2 image URLs + a list of difference coordinates + difficulty, editable via the admin panel, unlimited going forward once built) — but needs one upfront engineering build for the interactive click-to-find/highlight mechanic (comparable in scope to how Sudoku needed its own one-time build among the Games). The real bottleneck is **content sourcing, not code**: unlike a riddle (write it in 30 seconds), a spot-the-difference pair needs an actual edited image pair — licensed image sets or manual editing — which is slower/costlier to produce than text puzzles. Do this after a plan exists for where the image pairs come from; also benefits from real image uploads (below) rather than paste-a-URL.
- [ ] **Friends-only leaderboards** — game leaderboards are currently global by nickname; a lightweight "share your profile link, see just each other's scores" friend view would need some kind of friend-linking, but no follow-graph infrastructure beyond that. Lowest priority here: narrowest audience of the four.

**Admin-side**
- [ ] Basic automated test suite (login, quiz-taking flow, friendship-quiz flow) — currently everything is verified by hand each session. Highest priority here: an engineering safety net that protects every other feature above, and the risk of a manual-only test process grows with the codebase, not shrinks.
- [ ] **Real image uploads via cloud storage** (Cloudinary recommended — 25GB free tier) — picture Puzzles currently use paste-a-URL since no persistent storage exists yet (Render's server disk is ephemeral, wiped on every redeploy, so a naive server-disk upload would silently break). Explicitly deferred by the owner (2026-08-05) until after Puzzles shipped with paste-a-URL (it has) — **requires the owner to create the Cloudinary account themselves first** (Claude can't sign up for third-party services on their behalf) and share the API credentials (Cloud Name/API Key/API Secret, ideally as Render env vars, not pasted in chat) before this can be built. Ranked below the test suite/comments since it's blocked on an owner action, not something that can be picked up and finished end-to-end right now.
- [ ] **Content approval workflow** — Editor submits, Superadmin approves before it goes live. Meaningful once there are more than a couple of active admins; today Editor can publish directly, which is fine at the current team size.
- [ ] **Community-submitted content with a moderation queue** — e.g. visitor-submitted jokes/quotes, reviewed before publishing. Genuinely needs accounts (to attribute submissions and rate-limit spam) plus a real moderation UI, not a quick add-on.
- [ ] **Fix: two different accounts logged in at once in the same browser can leak stats between them** — found 2026-08-20 while verifying Referral rewards, not user-reported. `localStorage`'s `twegleStats` key is a single global key, not scoped per-account — if two different accounts are ever logged into two different tabs of the same browser at the same time, each one's 20-second background stats sync can read the *other* account's locally-cached numbers and merge them in (via the existing `Math.max`/OR merge in `statsSync.js`), inflating that field going forward for both. This is a rare scenario in real usage (a referrer and a new friend are essentially always on separate devices — the two roles this feature is actually built around), and the server-authoritative referral fields specifically were verified immune to it (only their unauthoritative *cached copy* could drift, self-healing on the next clean login). Every other stat field, though, has no such protection and could drift the same way for anyone who keeps two Twegle accounts open in one browser (e.g. a shared family computer). Low priority given how narrow the trigger is, but a real latent bug — proper fix would be namespacing `twegleStats` (and the other synced keys) by account/token rather than one shared key.

Deliberate design choice, caught during planning: kept the two referral facts as dedicated top-level `EndUser` fields, written only by the signup controller and overlaid into the computed stats at every read site, rather than trusting the client-synced stats blob for them — that blob gets overwritten wholesale on every sync push with no server merge, so storing the credit only there risked it being silently wiped by an unrelated sync race.
